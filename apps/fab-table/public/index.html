How it works
1. Print QR stickers for your deck from its fabbazaar.app deck page (More → QR sticker sheet) and sleeve them.
2. Create a table here and send the link to your opponent.
3. Scan the on-screen QR with your phone — it becomes your playmat camera. Mount it landscape above the mat.
